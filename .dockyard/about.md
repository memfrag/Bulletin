A macOS RSS reader whose sidebar is made of queries you wrote rather than folders
you filed things into.

Bulletin fetches feeds itself — no service account and no server. A stream is a
saved query, evaluated live every time you look at it, so nothing is ever filed
or changed to make it match. Feeds that send only the first paragraph are read in
full: Bulletin fetches the page and pulls the article out of it, and remembers
which approach works for which feed.

Search covers article text, not just headlines. The same story from five feeds
collapses to one row. Everything is reachable from the keyboard, and everything
reachable from the keyboard is also in a menu.
