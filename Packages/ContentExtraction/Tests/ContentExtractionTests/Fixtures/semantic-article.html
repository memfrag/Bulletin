How Feeds Got Truncated

Publishers discovered, some time in the middle of the last decade, that a feed carrying the whole article was a feed that never sent anyone to the site. The obvious response was to send a paragraph and a link, and so that is what a great many of them did, and the practice spread.

The result is that a reader which shows only what the feed contains is, for most of the web, a reader that shows only headlines. This is why every serious reader learned to go and fetch the page itself, and why the ones that did not eventually stopped being used.

What follows from that is a set of tradeoffs about privacy, speed, and how much of a browser you are willing to ship inside your application, none of which have a single correct answer.
